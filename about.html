About PeachtreeChat

PeachtreeChat is an Atlanta-based AI agency that helps local business owners use AI chatbots, AI assistants, and AI receptionists to capture leads, answer questions, book appointments, handle intake, and follow up with customers faster.

We build practical automation for salons, law firms, real estate teams, home service companies, and small businesses across Atlanta and Gwinnett County that want better customer response without hiring a full-time tech team.
